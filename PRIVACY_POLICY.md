# Privacy Policy for DiskVision

**Effective Date:** July 26, 2026

DiskVision ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy applies to the DiskVision macOS application.

## Data Collection and Usage

DiskVision is designed as a privacy-first, fully local desktop utility.

1. **No Personal Data Collection:** DiskVision does NOT collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, usage metrics, device identifiers, or user data.
2. **Local Processing Only:** All disk scanning, file size computations, and treemap/sunburst visual representations take place strictly offline on your local computer. No file contents, directory structure details, or file paths are ever uploaded to remote servers.
3. **App Sandbox:** DiskVision complies with Apple macOS App Sandbox requirements and only accesses directories explicitly selected by you via standard system folder pickers.

## Third-Party Services

DiskVision does not use third-party analytics, tracking tools, advertisement SDKs, or external databases.
